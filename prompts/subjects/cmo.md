## SUBJECT

a side-swept fringe, the front sections drawn back smoothly. Small hoop earrings
made as handcrafted props in matte muted gold, with no metallic glint. A subtle,
poised, closed-mouth smile with a soft mauve lip — the most polished and
presentational of the group.

She wears a mid-grey tailored blazer with a fine felted check, over a black top.
