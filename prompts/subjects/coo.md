## SUBJECT

A man in his mid thirties. Short black hair swept back with noticeable volume at
the front. Light stubble across the jaw and a short moustache-and-chin beard kept
close. A broad, confident, energetic smile with the teeth showing — the most
outgoing expression among the executives, though still short of a laugh.

He wears a deep slate-navy blazer, muted and desaturated to sit inside the series
palette, over a cream open-collar shirt.
