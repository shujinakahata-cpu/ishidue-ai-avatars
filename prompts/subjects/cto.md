# CTO

- 参照元: `cto.jpg` — **実写風**。フェルト風で作り直しが必要
- 参照元の背景（会議室）は使わない。背景は共通スタイルに従う
- 眼鏡もフェルト/針金の手作り小物として作る。金属光沢を出さない

## SUBJECT

A man in his mid thirties. Black hair worn slightly longer and tousled on top,
falling loosely over the forehead. Rectangular black-framed glasses, the frames
made as a handcrafted prop — matte felted or fine wire, with no metallic
glint. Clean-shaven, with a lean face. A quiet, thoughtful, closed-mouth smile;
the most reserved expression of the group.

He wears a mid-grey blazer with a visible felted weave over a soft blue chambray
button-down shirt.
